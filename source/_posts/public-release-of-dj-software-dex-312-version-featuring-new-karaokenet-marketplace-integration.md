## Public Release of DJ Software DEX 3.12 Version: Featuring New Karaoke.net Marketplace Integration!

[![New in DEX 3.12 Karaoke Song Store](https://i1.wp.com/pcdj.com/wp-content/uploads/2018/11/karaoke-dex3-new-cover.jpg?resize=706%2C321&ssl=1)](https://i1.wp.com/pcdj.com/wp-content/uploads/2018/11/karaoke-dex3-new-cover.jpg?fit=706%2C360&ssl=1 "New in DEX 3.12 Karaoke Song Store")

**Public Beta DEX 3.12 is ready for download and includes a new built-in karaoke song store for on-the-fly a la carte purchases.** 

[VIEW LARGER IMAGE ![Karaoke.net Song Store in DEX 3](https://i0.wp.com/pcdj.com/wp-content/uploads/2018/11/new-song-store-1.jpg?fit=300%2C300&ssl=1 "Karaoke.net Song Store in DEX 3")](https://i0.wp.com/pcdj.com/wp-content/uploads/2018/11/new-song-store-1.jpg?fit=516%2C514&ssl=1)

DEX 3.12 is a public beta, which means we’ve tested it thoroughly in house — now it’s your turn to take it for a test spin.

The big addition to [DEX 3](https://tools.techidaily.com/pcdj/products/) version 3.12 is the **Karaoke.net** Karaoke Store; Browse, Search, and Purchase karaoke song on-the-fly from within the DEX 3 browser. Purchase karaoke tracks a la carte or add tracks to the built-in shopping cart and checkout all at once. Purchased tracks are immediately downloaded and added to the new “Purchases” sub-tab located under the Karaoke.net tab within the browser pane so you can add them to singer rotation and play them straight away. All songs are yours to keep, and are downloaded directly to your hard drive in either Zipped MP3+G or MP4 (HD) format.

[VIEW LARGER IMAGE ![](https://i2.wp.com/pcdj.com/wp-content/uploads/2018/11/new-song-store-2.jpg?fit=300%2C138&ssl=1 "Karaoke store built into DEX 3")](https://i2.wp.com/pcdj.com/wp-content/uploads/2018/11/new-song-store-2.jpg?fit=880%2C406&ssl=1)

Subscribers to the [Party Tyme Karaoke subscription](https://tools.techidaily.com/pcdj/products/) already have access to the same karaoke tracks included in the Karaoke.net store. The new Karaoke.net store will greatly appeal to karaoke hosts with their own karaoke music collections that need to purchase karaoke song requests on-the-fly at events.

Since version 3.12 is considered a ‘[Public Beta](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Software%5Frelease%5Flife%5Fcycle)‘ you will want to test thoroughly before going live at your next event. With that said, our team has tested the new release extensively and found the new store to work as designed. If you happen to run into any issues please [join the Official PCDJ User Group](https://tools.techidaily.com/pcdj/products/) and report the problems there directly. If you need to roll back to the prior public release you can do so from the [DEX 3 support page](https://tools.techidaily.com/pcdj/products/).
