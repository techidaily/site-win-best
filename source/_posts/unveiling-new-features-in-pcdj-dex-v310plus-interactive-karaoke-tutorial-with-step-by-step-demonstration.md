DEX 3.10 ([currently in public beta as a release candidate](https://tools.techidaily.com/pcdj/products/)) was launched yesterday and comes with a series of new karaoke features designed to make it easier than ever to host a professional karaoke show.

In the video above we demonstrate the new karaoke singer rotation list, singer and song history, and more.
