## Mastering Video Production: Editing Techniques with Pioneer CDJ Dexterity Version 3.2

[![](https://i2.wp.com/pcdj.com/wp-content/uploads/2015/01/recordyourvideomix-dex3.jpg?resize=530%2C298&ssl=1)](https://i2.wp.com/pcdj.com/wp-content/uploads/2015/01/recordyourvideomix-dex3.jpg?fit=530%2C298&ssl=1 "recordyourvideomix-dex3")

Our flagship **DJ mixing software**, PCDJ DEX 3, just received a few new features and updates last week with the [launch of the 3.2 version](https://tools.techidaily.com/pcdj/products/).

The most notable changes are true 64bit Windows Support (_Windows 7 and Windows 8/8.1_), support for Direct X 11 and the ability to **record your video mix.** This last item is what we’ll focus on today.

Video mixing is more popular than ever, and provides DJ mixing video a potential leg-up on the competition. The total multimedia experience can help you carve out a niche and receive referrals for gigs other DJs aren’t geared to handle. One way to help promote your video mixing abilities is to record a video mix and send to your client.

DEX 3.2 now makes this as easy as recording audio was in previous versions. With a click of a button you can capture your video mix set in either SD (standard definition) or HD (high definition) in real-time.
